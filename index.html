Good to know

Questions, answered.

Need more information? Contact our team and we'll point you in the right direction.

Contact the clinic ↗
How do I book an appointment?

Use the appointment request form on this page or call the clinic. A team member will confirm your requested time. Online requests are not confirmed until the clinic contacts you.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Bring a photo ID, your insurance information if applicable, a list of current medications and any relevant medical records or test results.

Can I request my medical records?

Yes. Contact the clinic team for the records request process and any required authorization forms.

What if I need urgent medical help?

This website is not an emergency service. If you may be experiencing a medical emergency, call your local emergency number or go to the nearest emergency department.

Do you accept my insurance?

Contact the clinic with your insurance details so the team can confirm current participation and explain any expected costs.
